H. PROCUREMENT NOTES
--------------------

1. Buy two complete nodes first and validate the point-to-point link.
2. After successful ESP32-S3 + SX1262 communication, scale to six nodes.
3. Verify the final approved Pakistan RF band before purchasing LoRa modules/antennas.
4. SX1262 standalone pricing is an estimate because a stable local listing was not verified.
5. Imported boards can be dramatically more expensive than local listings.
6. Check whether shipping/customs are included before comparing prices.
7. Keep at least one spare ESP32-S3/radio if the project budget allows.
8. OLED, GPS, solar and final enclosures should be purchased later unless specifically required.
